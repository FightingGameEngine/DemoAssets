Unofficial History: Genis-Vell was born from the genes of the then recently deceased Mar-Vell. Raised by his mother Elysius, and implanted with a false identity, Genis grew to manhood and was eventually pulled back in time to the present where he now shares a bond with Rock Jones, the same man who had adventured with Mar-Vell, years earlier.

Normal Moves:

1) Crouch HP = Launch
2) Forward, HP (close) = Pommel (Throw)


Special Moves:

1) Down, Forward, LP = Crouch Beam
2) Down, Forward, LP = Stand Beam
3) Down, Forward, LP = Diagonal Beam
4) Foward, Down, Forward = Teleport
5) Down, Back, Kick = Ground Pound
6) Down, Forward, Kick = "Taste my power" Charge

Hyper Moves:

1) Down, Forward, 2 Punches = Cosmic Beam
2) Down, Back, 2 Kicks = Multi Charge
3) Down, Back, 2 Punches = Cosmic Explosion
