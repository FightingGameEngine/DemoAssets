==============
Unofficial History
==============
A new villain appeared in New York City, with the sole intent on controlling the local organized crime. The Green Goblin was born and he attempted to show his superiority by destroying Spider-Man. Although the Goblin never succeeded, he had learned the true identify of Spider-Man was Peter Parker. Afterwards The Green Goblin revealed himself to be Norman Osborn, the father of Peter's best friend, Harry. Peter helped to cure Norman of his Psychosis, only to have it return time and time again until the final confrontation where The Green Goblin captured the love of Peter's life, Gwen Stacey. Spider-man faced down the Goblin only to have Gwen throw off of a bridge and killed. Spider-man, in a rage, defeated Norman only to spare his life. Normal in turn attempted to kill Peter, only to succeed in taking his own. This couldn't change the fact that Gwen was gone, or that Norman’s son, Harry, blamed Spider-Man for his father's death.

Controls Key
==============
Down    		= D
Forward 		= F 
Up     			= U  
Back  			= B 
Down-Forward 		= DF,F
Down-Back 		= DB
Up-Forward 		= UF
Up-Back 		= UB
Hold for 2 seconds	= Hold
Start			= S

Any Punch		= P
Any Kick		= K

Light Punch 	(x) 	= LP
Medium Punch	(y) 	= MP
Heavy Punch 	(z) 	= HP
Light Kick  	(a) 	= LK 
Medium Kick  	(b) 	= MK
Heavy Kick   	(c) 	= HK

Two Punches 		= PP
Two Kicks   		= KK

==============
Game Play System
==============
* F,F 			= Dash Forward (on the ground or in the air)
* B,B			= Dash Backwards
* D,U			= Super Jump
* F while recovering 	= Forward Recovery Roll
* B while recovering	= Back Recovery Roll
* PP (While guarding)	= Guard Push
* S			= Taunt

==============
Normal Moves
==============

1) HP 			= Launch
2) Down HK 		= Machine Gun
3) Jump HK 		= Head Stomp
4) Forward HP 		= Throw


==============
Special Moves
==============

1) D,F,P 		= Pumpkin Bomb (high)
2) D,F,K 		= Pumpkin Bomb (low)
3) D,B,P	 	= Lazer
3) D,B,LK 		= Grapling Line
4) D,B,MK 		= Punch Line
5) D,B,HK 		= Electric Line


==============
Hyper Moves
==============

1) D,F,PP		= Pumpkin Barrage 
2) D,F,KK		= Glider Drive
3) D,B,PP		= Goblin's Attack
3) D,B,KK		= Goblin Wire
