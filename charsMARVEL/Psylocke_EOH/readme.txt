---=INTRODUCTION=-----------------------------------------------------------------------

First off, let me thank you for downloading my latest work, Psylocke. Being a fan of Psylocke 
during the '90s when I used to read comics, as well as when I used play her in video games she 
appears in, I really jumped on the chance to convert her to mugen for the sake of Super MvC: 
Eternity of Heroes. Being my fourth character I've made for mugen, I got a chance to work on 
improving my coding with her; since she's been made for Capcom, there's already a "standard" on
how she's to play MvC style, so I had to use my limited coding skills to try and not stray too 
far off course.

Since Psylocke's exclusively designed for the EOH project, I didn't want to just do a simple 
conversion of her, since a few creators have masterly done this already. So, I created her with
many extra features and few personal updates, with stuff I felt would be appropriate...if not 
cool. Most of my ideas came from X-Men: Next Dimension for the PS2 (a fun game I must say), 
although the ideas implemented may not resemble the original source at all. The end product, 
Psylocke is still quick & combo-friendly, but has improved defense and more WOW! factor. 

Hope eveyrone enjoys playing her outside the SMvC: EOH game. Enjoy!!!
