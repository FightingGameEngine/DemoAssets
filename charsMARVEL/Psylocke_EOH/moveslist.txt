
Moveslist for Psylocke - Super MvC: EOH Version 


---KEY NOTATION=---------------------------------------------------------------------------

F  = Forward           x = LP (Light Punch)          a = LK (Light Kick) 

B  = Back              y = MP (Medium Punch)         b = MK (Medium Kick)   

D  = Down              z = HP (Heavy Punch)          c = HK (Heavy Kick)

U  = Up                s = Start 


2P / 3P = Any Two Punches / All Three Punches 

2K / 3K = Any Two Kicks / All Three Kicks    



---=BASIC SYSTEM=------------------------------------------------------------------------

Jump Up         = U 

Jump Forward    = UF

Jump Backwards  = UF

Walk Forward    = Hold F

Walk backward   = Hold B

Crouch          = Hold D             

High Guard      = Hold B as opponent attacks

Low Guard       = Hold DB as opponent attacks

Dash Forward    = F, F 

Back Dash       = B, B        

Air Recovery    = x+y when falling in midair

Ground Recovery = x+y when falling near the ground



---=MVC SYSTEM=----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Advancing Guard	    = PP (Ground or Air)

Aerial Rave 	    = U, UF, or UB after opponent is launched to perform an air combo

Escape Roll - Short = Hold F (Forward) or B (Backward) 

Escape Roll - Long  = PP (Forward) or KK (Backward)

Variable Counter    = B,DB,D + P or K while blocking (Ground or air, uses 500 pts from the Super 
		      Guage) 

Super Jump          = D, U

Launcher            = D+HP or Vanity Flip (see the Combos section for more about this)

OTG Combo Starter   = D+LK 


---=ADVANCED MOVES=-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Dodge Kick     - B+HK

Heel Drop      - F+MK

Evasive Roll   - Hold Forward during standing HK or Dodge Kick

Moonsault Kick - U+HK (Can perform Triple Moonsault Kick through this)

Vanity Flip    - B+HK (see the Combos section on more about this)

Triple Jump    - U or UB or UF twice while in midair or after jumping off the ground


---=THROWS=------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GROUND
------

Hell Ride - Close, F+HP

Toss-Up   - Close, F+HK


AIR
---

Revolver  - Close, F+HP 


---=SPECIAL MOVES=-----------------------------------------------------------------------

1) PSI-BLAST - D,DF,F + P (Ground/Air)
   --------
Desc: Blast of psychic energy. Button pressed determines direction of blast.

LP - Down
MP - Forward
HP - Up 


2) PSI-BLADE SPIN - D,DF,F + K, press a different K strength to chain more hits (Up to 3 times) (Ground/Air)
   --------------
Desc: Spinning attack with psychic knives on Psylocke's hands.  Button pressed determines 
      direction of spin.

LK - Diagonal-Up
MK - Straight Forward
HK - Straight Up


3) NINJUTSU - F,D,B + P or K, Press any button & direction to perform a Thrust Attack (Ground/Air)
   --------
Desc: Psylocke teleports at different points around her opponent, Button determines position 
      of reappearance.

LP; High, before of opponent
MP: Next to opponent 
HP: High, behind opponent
LK: Low, before opponent
MK: Same location
HK: Low, behind opponent


4) HAYATE NO JUTSU - B,D,DB + P (Ground)
   ---------------
Desc: Dodge & rush attack. Button determines distance of dodge and rush respectively. Can 
      follow up with any of the choices below under the required conditions:
				
<During Dodge Roll>          <After Rush Punch hits>       <During Flip Over (midair)>
a) Hold 2P (Cascade Rush)    a) P (Pierce)         	   a) P (Over Strike)
b) Hold 2K (Catapult)        b) K (Spin Chain) 
                             c) Hold back (Retreat)
                             d) Hold up (Flip Over)
                             e) Hold Down (Duck Down)
           
<During Flip Over (Land & no attack)>          <During Duck Down>
a) Hold 2P (Cascade Rush)                      a) P (Launcher)
b) Hold 2K (Catapult)                          b) K (Sweep)                
                                               c) Hold back (Dodge Kick)
                                               d) Hold forward (Evasive Roll)
                                               e) Hold Up (Flip Over)

<During Dodge Kick (landing)                   <Dodge Kick hits>
a) Hold 2P (Cascade Rush)                      a) Hold Forward (Evasive Roll) 
b) Hold 2K (Catapult)
                                       

---=HYPER COMBO LVL 1 (Requires 1 bar in the Super Meter)=-------------------------------------

1) PSI-THRUST - D,DF,F + 2P (Ground/Air)
   ----------
Desc: Powerful psi-knife thrust attack. Change direction/extend attack by rapidly pressing any P
      button.

2) KOUCHOU GAKURE - D,DB,B + 2K (Ground/Air)
   --------------
Desc: Multiple phychic butterflies encircling Psylocke. Direction held will determine butterflies'
      departure path:

Nothing   : 4-directional split
Right     : Fly forward
Left      : Fly behind Psylocke 
Up        : Fly up
Down      : Fly down
Right-Up  : Fly diag. up-forward
Right-Down: Fly diag. down-forward
Left-Up   : Fly diag. up-back
Left-Down : Fly diag down-back


3) PSYCHIC VEIL - D, D + 2K (Ground)
   ------------
Desc: Covering her eyes Psylocke will counter her opponent from any direction. Outcome of counter
      Depends on opponent's position:

a) Doppelganger (Opponent's on the ground)
b) Tornado (Opponent's in the air)   


---=HYPER COMBO LVL 2 (Requires 2 bars in the Super Meter)=-------------------------------------

1) PSI-MALESTROM - D,DF,F + 2K, press K rapidly to add more hits & damage (Ground)
   -------------
Desc: Super spinnig psi-knife attack, all hits will connect upon contact.


2) ILLUSION - F,D,B + 2P (random invincibility, cannot use other Hypers during this)
   --------
Desc: Psylocke emits a psychic pulse, making the opponent see multiple images of her. Attacks
      towards Psylocke will randomly miss. Lasts for a limited amount of time.


---=INFINITY HYPER COMBO (Requires 3 bars in the Super Meter)=---------------------------------

1) CONCUSSION - B,D,DB + 2P 
   ----------
Desc: Psychic knife stab that induces immobilization and neutralizes her opponent's senses, 
      leaving him/her open for a psychic slash assault.


---=CHAIN COMBOS=-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Chain Type = L-M-H, P-K, Air and Ground, Standing-Crouching, Crouching-Standing, and Air to Ground

Moonsault Kick = Can chain into itself 3 times for Triple Moon Kick (hold U & rapidly press HK)   


---=CANCEL SYSTEM=-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Basic-to-Specials = All basics (except Crouch HK) can cancel into all Specials

Basic-to-Hypers = All basics (except Crouch HK) can cancel into all Hypers except Concussion and
                  Psychic Veil*. 

Heel Drop = Can hypercancel into Kouchou Gakure and Ps-Thrust

Triple Moonasault Kick = Can hypercancel into Kouchou Gakure and Ps-Thrust during 1st or 2nd 
                         Moonsault only)

Vanity Flip = Can only hypercancel into Psi-Thrust and Kouchou Gakure   


Specials-to-Hypers = The following can cancel into Hypers (except Concussion and Psychic Veil):
                     a) Psi-Blade Spin (only initial attack, if chained into another Psi-Blade 
                        variant you cannot cancel into Hypers; cannot cancel into Psi-Malestrom)
                     b) Hayate no Jutsu (Pierce, Sweep, 1st part of Spin Chain, and Launcher 
                        only)

*Note that you can cancel into Psychic Veil through Forward Dash


---=VANITY FLIP=-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Vanity Flip can only be accessed through certain combos Here's the possible means to using 
Vanity Flip:

STAND                           CROUCH                        AIR
-----                           ------                        ---
LP-B+HK                         LP-B+HK                       LP (after landing), B+HK
LP-MP-B+HK                      LP-MP-B+HK
LP-LK-B+HK                      LP-LK-B+HK
LP-HP-B+HK                      LP-HP-B+HK
LP-HK-B+HK                      LP-HK-B+HK
LP-LK-MP-MK-B+HK                LP-LK-MP-MK-B+HK

Standing and crouching combos can be mixed together and still execute Vanity Flip. While jumping, 
if LP is pressed right before landing or the only attack down while in the air, Psylocke will 
going into a special landing state that allows her to execute Vanity Flip on its own. Vanity Flip 
acts like a launcher upon the 2nd hit, giving Psylocke the ability to super jump after her opponent 
and go into an Aerial Rave.


---=NOTES=-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-Forward Dash and Back Dash have slight invincibility upon start up.

-Cascade Rush and Catapult are special throws that are only executable during Hayate no Jutsu.

-The opponent can recover around the peak of their flight up after being thrown by Catapult.

-Opponent can recover after being thrown by Hell Ride

-Cascade Rush has two outcomes. If it connects with an opponent on the ground, it becomes its true outcome 
 (Cascade Rush). However, if it connects with an airborne opponent, then it becomes Revolver.

-Psylocke has random invincibility during Illusion, which changes after a set period of time. 
 She will gain invincibility to physical attacks and projectiles for 15 ticks in intervals of 40 
 ticks, while she'll be invincible to throws for 30 ticks in intervals of 50 ticks. Note that her
 invincibilty to projectiles and physical attacks do not take place at the same time, but work when 
 the other's not in play. Also, if Psylocke is hit at anytime, her invincibility will become null 
 until she fully recovers. 

-Psi-Thrust can change directions if Psylocke runs into a wall or the floor of the stage only if you
 didn't extend Psi-Thrust ahead of time. Also, holding in the opposite direction after Psi-Thrust 
 finishes allows Psylocke to leap away and gain distance (only if she's some distance away from the ground).

-Psychic Veil is a counter Hyper, where the opponent needs to attack her in order for it to initiate.
 Psylocke can only counter against normal, special, and hyper physical attacks; projectiles and 
 throws of all types will do damage on her. On top of that, Psychic Veil has room for error, for it 
 can miss even if Psylocke successfully counters her opponent's attack. 

-If Psychic Veil counters an airborne opponent's attack, Psylocke will do the counter Tornado, which
 spirals her opponent & leaves them open for a followup attack. On the other hand, if a grounded 
 opponent's attack is countered, Psylocke will perform Doppelganger, a multihit attack. Both versions 
 have their strong points, so learn how to use them well.

-Although Hayate no Jutsu has many followups, it is not too confusing to use. Just keep in mind that 
 after inputting a followup you cannot do another one except for a few cases. If you do Duck Down 
 after Psylocke hits the opponent with the Rush Punch, you can follow up with either an Evasive Roll,
 Dodge Kick, Sweep, Launcher, or a Flip Over. During Dodge Kick, just as Psylocke lands, you can 
 interrupt it through Cascade Rush or Catapult.

-During Hayate no Jutsu, if you perform Flip Over, you either have the option of doing an air attack
 or Cascade Rush or Catapult. If you do the Over Strike, you cannot do any of the throws once Psylocke
 lands, for both throws are only executable if Psylocke stays in the Flip Over throughout.
