==============
Unofficial History
==============
Johnny Storm, after being doused in cosmic radiation following a test flight into space,
found he had the ability to alight his body in flames without getting burnt. Along with
his Sister the Invisible Woman, her husband Mr. Fantastic, and the ever lovin' blue eyed
Thing make up the Fantastic Four, New York’s favorite super hero team.

==============
Controls Key
==============
Down    		= D
Forward 		= F 
Up     			= U  
Back  			= B 
Down-Forward 		= DF
Down-Back 		= DB
Up-Forward 		= UF
Up-Back 		= UB
Hold for 2 seconds	= Hold
Start			= S

Any Punch		= P
Any Kick		= K

Light Punch 	(x) 	= LP
Medium Punch	(y) 	= MP
Heavy Punch 	(z) 	= HP
Light Kick  	(a) 	= LK 
Medium Kick  	(b) 	= MK
Heavy Kick   	(c) 	= HK

Two Punches 		= PP
Two Kicks   		= KK

==============
Game Play System
==============
* F,F 			= Dash Forward (on the ground or in the air)
* B,B			= Dash Backwards (on the ground or in the air)
* D,U			= Super Jump
* U			= Double Jump (while jumping)
* F while recovering 	= Forward Recovery Roll
* B while recovering	= Back Recovery Roll
* PP (While guarding)	= Guard Push
* S			= Taunt
* Hold U		= Hover (in the air)

==============
Normal Moves
==============
* D,HP			= Launch
* D,HK 			= Trip
* F,HP			= Throw

==============
Special Moves
==============
1) D,F,P		= Fire Ball
2) D,B,P		= Plasma Blast
3) D,F,K		= Flame Trap
4) D,B,K		= Flame Ball
5) P (Rapidly)		= Flame Punch

==============
Hyper Moves
==============
1) D,F,PP 		= Nova Blast (on the ground or in the air)
2) D,B,PP		= Infinity Flame
3) D,B,KK		= Hyper Flame Ball (on the ground or in the air)

==============
Palettes
==============
1 = Orange
2 = Red
3 = Yellow
4 = Orange Gradient
5 = Red Gradient
6 = Dark Red
