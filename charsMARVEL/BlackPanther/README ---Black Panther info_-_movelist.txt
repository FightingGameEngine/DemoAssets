T'challa AKA The Black Panther

This is the first release of The Black Panther, I've had three creators help me for the past 12 months and i've been slowly working him, bit by bit,
i felt the need to keep as much Vibranium influence as i could, with his specials, and make him as althetic and speedy, 
i also didn't want to overdo the claw moves, so i kept that as simple as possible, without making him a generic slasher character like wolverine or sabretooth
thanks goes out to hypersonic92, nicotinefist and alan joestar (with some of the start sprites and basework, and conceptions that helped me make this posssible)
also thank harbinger for aquiring of the sound files for black panther from MUA, which i have since edited to deepen his voice somewhat, 
plus cutting up of some lengthy speeches, 

and a big kudos to Acey for helping cure my coding issues, and with the template help and general guidance

Pounce = D DB B + K (also in Air)

Vibranium Echo  =  D DB B + K
