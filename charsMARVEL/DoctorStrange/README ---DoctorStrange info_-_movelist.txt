 Doctor Steven Strange

This is the first release of The Doctor Strange, and a very long time coming, I've had many creators donate a few images, when we started this wip, 
but in the end all was remade and re-edited to his final look (the magneto-base which angelus had managed to sprite the bulk of)
Please thank him for that, otherwise this wip would still be a bunch of random images, his work on detailing the cape also very draining but worth the effort
i wanted to put as much of his enchantments as i could, but really wanted also to have him randomly choosing them also, this was a hard thing to organise
Volzilla also did his best to aquire as many effects possible to show these enchantments, in the end i decided to keep the moves quite simple
pooling all the dimensional ideas into one hyper attack, making a counter hyper of "spells of the vishanti" and randomising that too 
and accessing a enchantment that would aid your attacks, and of course the standard maelstrom hyper blast 
thanks goes out to Angelus Silverhead and Volzilla, (with some of the start sprites and basework, AI and conceptions that helped me make this posssible)

A big kudos to Acey for helping cure my coding issues, and with the template help and general guidance
and Loganir for the animation and Stage sprites regarding Asgard in the conjurers cone hyper, 
aswell as any creators that help create the stages used
