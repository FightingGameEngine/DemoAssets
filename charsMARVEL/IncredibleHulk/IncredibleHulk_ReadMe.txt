==============
Unofficial History
==============
Dr. Bruce Banner, after being bathed in waves of Gamma Radiation was transformed into the Incredilbe Hulk. To this day the Hulk is one of the most powerful adversaries in the world, for good and for bad.

==============
Controls Key
==============
Down    		= D
Forward 		= F 
Up     			= U  
Back  			= B 
Down-Forward 		= DF,F
Down-Back 		= DB
Up-Forward 		= UF
Up-Back 		= UB
Hold for 2 seconds	= Hold
Start			= S

Any Punch		= P
Any Kick		= K

Light Punch 	(x) 	= LP
Medium Punch	(y) 	= MP
Heavy Punch 	(z) 	= HP
Light Kick  	(a) 	= LK 
Medium Kick  	(b) 	= MK
Heavy Kick   	(c) 	= HK

Two Punches 		= PP
Two Kicks   		= KK

==============
Game Play System
==============
* F,F 			= Dash Forward (on the ground or in the air)
* B,B			= Dash Backwards
* D,U			= Super Jump
* F while recovering 	= Forward Recovery Roll
* B while recovering	= Back Recovery Roll
* PP (While guarding)	= Guard Push
* S			= Taunt

==============
Normal Moves
==============
1) D,HP 		= Launch
2) D,HK 		= Trip
3) HP or HK 		= Knock Down
4) F,HP (close) 	= Throw

==============
Special Moves
==============
1) D,DF,F,P		= Gamma Charge
2) D,DF,F,K 		= Aerial (Diagonal) Gamma Charge
3) F,D,DF,P		= Aerial (up) Gamma Charge
4) Hold B,F,P 		= Ground Wave
5) D,B,P 		= Gamma Hurricane

==============
Hyper Moves
==============
1) D,F,PP 		= Gamma Tsunami
2) D,B,PP 		= Gamma Crush
3) D,F,KK 		= Gamma Quake
