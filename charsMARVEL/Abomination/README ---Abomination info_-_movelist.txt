Emil Blonsky AKA The Abomination

This is the first release of The Abomination, been wanting to see a real villian for the unstoppable and incredible Hulk, well now it's real, 
and hopefully what has been expected.
Cravd one of the most Unique spriters i've ever work with, had worked along she-hulk prior to this and i can say and his skills are amazing, 
makes my life so much easier, Unfortunetly i couldn't continue she-hulk, but this hopefully opens a whole new genre in villians, 
and I'm looking to find more marvel foes to work on in the future (hint, hint creators)
Been struggling to figure out what moves he is capable of regarding either grabs or scratching/ slicing or just plain pummeling, but with playing Marvel lego heroes
and see other media, come up with a general moveset, that defines Emil's powerset

Now Smart palleting this Crazy Loon, was very....... educational, to say the least, i managed to learn how to add lines to a character without harming the original sprite.
That means you get to see Classic Cravd Abombination, and also Current Arkady Abomination, all rolled into one, (this was very time consuming, but worth it)
and i hope you enjoy this Giant character that is Emil Blonsky, and his somewhat sidekick The Leader (Bwahahaha)

Thanks goes out Cravd for his exceptional spritework and detailing and also to Volzilla who's persistence in getting creators together, and is what helped this WIP come about, 
and a big Thanks to Acey and Zvitor for helping my coding issues by checking out through their combined works, & with the template help and general guidance, 
Also hypersonic's great voice work, which makes this character come alive in his violent tone, that only hypersonic could do.
