==============
Unofficial History
==============
Raised as the half brother of the X-men's Professor Charles Xavier, Cain Marko grew to loath his brother's preferential treatment. Cain was eventually sent away to a military boarding school follow by service in the Korean War. Cain was stationed with his brother Charles. Due to a series of circumstances the brothers found themselves trapped together in a cave that contained the Crimson Gem of Cyttorak. Cain took possession of the crystal which lead to the cave collapsing. Charles escaped but Cain was left until the mountain debris, only to dig himself out year later with a burning hatred for Charles. Meantime, the crystal had turned Cain into the unstoppable Juggernaut. In time Juggernaut joined forces with Black Tom Cassidy, the cousin of the X-Men's Banshee. After year and years of battling against his brother's X-Men, Cain eventually reformed and joined the X-Men, later forming a new reincarnation of Excalibur.

==============
Game Play System
==============
* F,F 			= Dash Forward
* B,B			= Dash Backwards
* D,U			= Super Jump
* U			= Double Jump (while jumping)
* F while recovering 	= Forward Recovery Roll
* B while recovering	= Back Recovery Roll
* PP			= Guard Push
* S			= Taunt
* Hold F		= Wall Cling (Near Wall)

==============
Normal Moves
==============
* HP or HK		= Knock Down
* F,HP 			= Throw (on the ground or in the air)
* F,HK 			= Throw
* D,HP			= Launcher
* D,HK 			= Trip
