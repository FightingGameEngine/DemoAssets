The Vision

Ultron created his "vision of the future" as an android with the ability to modify his density, 
becoming either intangible or super-strong at a moment's notice. With the help of S.H.I.E.L.D., 
Vision broke free from his evil creator and joined the Avengers. Despite his analytical mind and artificial origins, 
he has proven himself to be a defender of humanity in all respects. 

kudos to Acey for some help with spritework also for helping and with the template help and general guidance
as without him, this character wouldn't be possible, and also HyperVoiceActing for adding some nice voice work,
along with other ideas to make The Vision even better
