MMV Blade Marvel by Fede de 10 and updated by borewood2013, based on mouser's MvsC MUGEN stuff.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------

MUGEN note #1:
- this MMV Blade Marvel is for use in MUGEN1.0 only

MUGEN note #2:
- in MsvC any character is allowed to walk during the in-game intro, in this MUGEN version Blade is also allowed to do so, under these MUGEN conditions:
  1) he must finish his MUGEN in-game intro animation first;
  2) he is allowed to walk only, and he cannot do anything else
* this small MUGEN feature is based on ⑨'s Melty Blood MUGEN way:
   http://nineball.webcrow.jp/etc.htm#３、開幕前の行動

MUGEN note #3:
- in the config.txt text file, besides mouser's original setting options, you can also choose a couple of MUGEN things:
  1) hyper portrait choice => choose between two (2) different hyper portraits
  2) rifle behavior choice => choose between two (2) different rifle behaviors

MUGEN note #4:
- The car sprite has been used in one (1) of the MUGEN win poses, rather than in a hyper move, because there is already a "Hyper Motorcycle Attack"

MUGEN note #5:
- This mugenized Blade has very few Blade voices as it is so difficult to try to find more Blade's voices.........

MUGEN note #6:
- This mugenized Blade has medium MUGEN A.I.

MUGEN move list:

- F, F on ground, to run forward
- B, B on ground, to hop backward
- D, U on ground, to super jump
- when blocking/guarding MUGEN Enemies' attacks, press any two (2) punches (MUGEN button "x+y"/"y+z"/"x+z") to do Advanced Guard (guard push)
- when falling in the air and touching the ground, press any two (2) punches (MUGEN button "x+y"/"y+z"/"x+z") to do Recover Roll forward

- close to a MUGEN Enemy, hold forward/backward and press hard punch/kick (MUGEN button "z"/"c") to throw MUGEN Enemy

- Special Moves:
  1) D, DF, F any punch (MUGEN button "x"/"y"/"z") on ground, to do Pistol Shoot
      * different punch presses for different aim angles
  2) D, DB, B any punch (MUGEN button "x"/"y"/"z") on ground, to do a Rush Slash
      * light punch version does not knock down a MUGEN Victim
      * medium punch version knocks down a MUGEN Victim
      * hard punch version deals two (2) hits
         => the 2nd second hit knocks down a MUGEN Victim (plus it must be standing blocked/guarded high)
  3) D, F, DF any punch (MUGEN button "x"/"y"/"z") on ground, to do Anti-air Slash
      * light punch version is fast but cannot cover much area
      * medium punch version can cover a bit more area by a short forward dash in advance
      * hard punch version deal two (2) hits and can cover a much more area by a longer forward dash in advance
  4) D, F, DF any punch (MUGEN button "x"/"y"/"z") in air, to do Aerial Pistol Shoot
      * different punch presses for different aim angles
  5) D, DF, F any kick (MUGEN button "a"/"b"/"c") on ground, to do Rifle Shoot
      * all button versions have the same start-up, during the start-up:
        => press light kick (MUGEN button "a") to aim low
        => press medium kick (MUGEN button "b") to aim high
        => press hard kick (MUGEN button "c") to aim diagonal upward
      !!!!!!!!! you can try to change the "rifle behavior choice" in the config.txt, to disable the start-up MUGEN feature
  6) D, DF, F any kick (MUGEN button "a"/"b"/"c") on air, to do Aerial Multi-hit Kick
      * all kick presses versions are the same

 - Hyper Moves (require and consume MUGEN Power 1000/3000):
  1) D, DF, F any two (2) punches (MUGEN button "x+y"/"y+z"/"x+z") on ground, to do Hyper Pistol Shoot
  2) D, DB, B any two (2) punches (MUGEN button "x+y"/"y+z"/"x+z") on ground, to do Hyper Gatling Gun Shoot
  3) D, DF, F any two (2) punches (MUGEN button "x+y"/"y+z"/"x+z") on air, to do Hyper Aerial Pistol Shoot
  4) D, DF, F any two (2) kicks (MUGEN button "a+b"/"b+c"/"a+c") on ground, to do Hyper Rush Combo
  5) D, DB, B any two (2) kicks (MUGEN button "a+b"/"b+c"/"a+c") on ground, to do Hyper Motorcycle Attack
  * all other Hyper Moves do not make Blade invincible, only the Hyper Motorcycle Attack gives total invincibility to Blade

- Combo-abilities:
   => chain combos and air combos by mouser's MvsC MUGEN way
   => special moves cancellable to hyper moves
