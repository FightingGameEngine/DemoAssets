===================================
COMMAND LIST
===================================

-------------
Specials:
-------------

Bamf! - Couldn't very well have a Nightcrawler without his Bamf! This move let's you teleport around the stage, the strength of the punch or kick will dictate how far he teleports.
D, DB, B + P or K

Teleport Attack - He not only bamfs, he can do it in style while attacking his enemy. A quick and fun way to send a surprise blow to your opponent.
D, DF, F + P

Sword Attack - Nightcrawler loves pretending he's Errol Flynn, do this to give the fight a taste of chivalry. The strength of the kick button dictates how many times he'll strike his enemy with his blade.
D, DF, F + K

-------------
Hypers:
-------------

Teleporter - This is what you've been waiting for, the move that let's you bamf all over the screen and whack your opponent all over the screen with you.
D, DF, F + 2P

Now You See me... - Now you see him, now you... oh snap, where'd he go! Yes, a possibly less known fact is Nightcrawler can fade into the shadows, allowing him to wreak havoc on his enemies with greater ease. Watch out for this one, cause you can't hit what you can't see.
D, DB, B + 2P

The Buccaneer - Now, this is Errol Flynn on steroids. Make Nightcrawler really happy and let him slice his enemy with his dual blades and end it with an epic final blow in the air!
D, DF, F + 2K

Excalibur (at power level 3) - Not that Nightcrawler actually needs any help, it's just fun to call some old friends over to gang up on your opponent. This is the BIG one, the real damage maker. Call the old Excalibur back for one final team attack.
D, DB, B + P and K
