Ultron-1 was constructed by Dr. Hank Pym of The Avengers as the famed scientist/adventurer was experimenting in 
high-intelligence robotics. Ultron became sentient and rebelled, hypnotizing Pym and brainwashing him into forgetting 
that Ultron had ever existed. He immediately began improving upon his rudimentary design, quickly upgrading himself 
several times to become Ultron-5. He then went on to organize the second incarnation of the Masters of Evil under 
the guise of the Crimson Cowl, and battled Pym and his teammates in the Avengers.

What is done? in Ultron
----------------------------------------------
Basics 
- Stance
- Turn
- Crouch
- Walk (forward/backwards)
- Dash (forward/backwards)
- Blocking
- Gethits/falling/KO
- Jump
- Jump forward/backward
- All standing punches/kicks
- All crouching punches/kicks
- All air punches/kicks
- Taunt
- Recovery roll (while knocked down just press F or B depending on what direction you want to go)
- Guard Push (while gethitting on defense D,DF + PP)
- Dizzy
- Lose by time
- Frozen state (triggered by Ice) 
- Electrocuted state (triggered by Blanka, Thor, Batman ....)
- Burned state (triggered by Ironman, Crystal, jill valentine, Batman...)
- 3 Intro
- 3 Win Poses
- Chain Combos
- 2 Throws
- 2 Modes

---------------------------------
First Mode "Adamantium":
---------------------------------
+ A small invisibility time on basics and higher priority on hits.
+ Super Armor
+ Ultron does more damage.
- Slow movement
- Less combos
Combo style is L, M, F (boss type)
When he is on AI he primarily stay still and wait for counter attacks.


Specials Adamantium Mode
-Laser Beam                      (D, DF + any P)
-Energy Drain Beam               (F, D, DF + any P)
-Ultron Crusher                  (D,DB + any P)
-Fury weapons                    (D, DF + any K)
-Heavy Stomp                     (On Air, D, DB + any K)
-Ground to Jump Stomp            (D, DB + any K)
-Fly (hover) Mode                (D, DB, B + LP+LK) 
Works on air too, Ultron is charging power while he is flying, if power bar is fill this move doesn't activate
in training mode the power bar is always fill for that reason this move does NOT work on training.

Hypers
-Encephalo Ray                   (QCF + PP)
-Hyper Crusher                   (QCB + PP)
-Fury                            (QCF + KK)
-Ultron vehicle                  (QCB + KK)

-Ultron Minions                  (QCF + FP+FK)
* First level  - Mini Ultrons
* Second level - Victor Mancha
* Third level  - Alkhema


*This is a combo hyper if hits,instrucctions will appear, if you succed in the secuence Ultron
will call more strikers to continue the hyper, if fails the hyper move will end after the first failed combination.

*For success in the 3 hyper you need 3000 of power if you have 2000 you can only combo 2 hypers if you only have 1000
only the first part will show and the command list wont even appear.

*When the arrows show up, it does not matter your position on the screen if <- appears it means left not back.
 
key:

<- left
-> right 
triangle pointing up is up
triangle pointing down obviously is down

LP Low Punch
MP Medium Punch
FP Fierce Punch or Strong Punch
LK Low Kick
MP Medium Punch
FP Fierce Kick or Strong Kick

*This is the order: 
Start the Ultron Minions Hyper (QCF + FP+FK) and if hits:
While the mini ultrons hit the enemy, the commands will appear at random this 2 are the posible combinations to follow into the next part:

Left, right, left, right, Fierce Kick 
or
Up, down, up, down, Fierce Kick

If succes the Victor Mancha jump in to assist, while it is executed again commands will appear, this are the posible combinations:

Right, up, Low punch, Low kick, Fierce punch
or
Low punch, Medium punch, Fierce punch, Medium Kick, Fierce punch

If succes the hyper combo will end with Alkhema shotting a very big beam.

*Take a breath, analize the commands quickly and press calmly, you will see that it is easy to do the full hyper combo.


---------------------------------
Second Mode "Gold":
---------------------------------
For get the mode: While selecting Ultron press start + any buton. 
- Recieves regular damage.
- No super Armor
- Ultron does less damage.
+ He is faster than adamantium mode
+ Crazy combo machine.
+ He can combo air hits and does a flying kick while running.
+ He can teleport.
When he is on AI he dash into the enemy looking to it for hit it.

Combo style is L, L, M, M, F (regular versus type)

Apparently is the same move set but there are sighly modifications maybe not noticibale by the common player.

Specials Gold Mode
-Laser Beam                      (D, DF + any P)
-Concussion Blasters             (F, D, DF + any P)
-Teleport hit                    (D,DB + any P)
-Fury weapons                    (D, DF + any K)
-Air ripper                      (On Air, D, DB + any K)
-Telepor to Ripper               (D, DB + any K)
-Fly (hover) Mode                (D, DB, B + LP+LK) 

Hypers
-Encephalo Ray                   (QCF + PP)
-Hyper Crusher                   (QCB + PP)
-Fury                            (QCF + KK)
-Ultron Vehicle                  (QCB + KK)

-Ultron Solo Operative           (QCF + FP+FK)
* First level  - Ultron Clones
* Second level - Metallic rain
* Third level  - Ultimate Ripper


The combo hyper from gold mode is visually different of Adamantium mode, but the commands are the same.
