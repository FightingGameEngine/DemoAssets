[The Unlimited] Proudly Present to You, A Marvel Comics MUGEN Creation:

-----------------------------------------------------
DEADPOOL!!! The World (In)Famous Merc with a Mouth!!!
-----------------------------------------------------
<Version 1.0> Christmas Gift to the MUGEN World!

----------
The Basics
----------
Wade Wilson is a 6 button character built in the
Capcom versus series tradition. Take that, you 
four button hacks! Two buttons mightier than you'll 
ever be!!!


--------------
Basic Attacks:
--------------

LP - A quick jab.
MP - Combat knife gash.
HP - Katana cross swipe.

LK - A knee to the gut.
MK - A swift side flat kick.
HK - A mighty leg lift. (Air combo launcher)

Crouch LP - A crouching jab.
Crouch MP - Nunchaku! Woo-ha! (Chain to launcher)
Crouch HP - A manly uppercut. (Air combo launcher)

Crouch LK - Slight slide kick.
Crouch MK - Mule kick! Hee-haw!
Crouch HK - Your standard sweep. (Knockdown)


Air LP - Another quick jab.
Air MP - Katan slash across.
Air HP - Wakazashi hammer swipe.(Knockdown)

Air LK - Light thrust kick.
Air MK - Ninja sunrise kick.
Air HK - Stompity stomp! (Knockdown)


----------------
Special Attacks:
----------------

Bomb Voyage - D,DF,F+K:
-- 'Pool throws one of his many bandoliered (is that a word?)
grenades. LK throws an incindiary (projectile damage,) MK 
throws a timefreeze (frozen opponent) and HK tosses a flashbang
(stunned opponent / knockdown on aerial opponent.) Rumor has it
you can hold down the kick button for a landmine styled trick-toss.

TLPRT - D,DB,B+Any:
-- Spider-Pool! Spider-Pool! Flash around the screen like... 
a Spider-Pool does! Anyway, the 6 buttons correspond to where on 
the play field he'll appear. Great for a quick approach or escape alike!

Gears of War - Charge B,F+P:
-- 'Pool whips out on of his many mercenary goodies and unloads.
LP is the long range short damage Uzi, MP is the midrange mid-damage 
dual pistols and HP is the short range high damage Shotgun.

Aerial Assault - D,DF,F+P (IN AIR):
-- More goodies for the lucky boys and girls! If this were a Christmas
release, I'd make a corny joke about Santa! Wait... Regardless, LP
throws a diagonal short range switchblade, MP hurls a horizontal shuriken,
and HP spreads three tricky kunai daggers.

Swords of AWESOME - D,DF,F+P:
-- One potato, two potato, three potato, NINJA! Slice and dice your 
enemies with a one, two or three swipe katana ground rush. The second
swipe of the MP version can be cancelled into aerial attacks,too.

Common Sense TINGLING! - Start:
-- Awww, I wasted the Spidey joke already. Oh well. Here's a great
multi-function move which cause Pool to point and taunt with one of
his favorite words. By taking this non-breather, you also gain back
a sliver of health. Not a grand recovery, but try TLPRTing behind an
opponent as they start a long super, and proceed to heal away!

SHORYUUUKEN! - F,D,DF+P:
-- See Capcom? It's spelled different, so... it's not the same thing.
It's like the fargate. As for an actual move description... really?
Do I really need to explain that you must defeat Sheng Long to stand a chance?


-----------
Super Arts:
-----------

TLPRT Killah! - D,DF,F+PP:
-- Wade lets loose with a crouching uppercut that leads into a pinball
volley of painful teleport swats. This has the quickest startup of any of
his supers, and makes a great punishment against an animation heavy attack,
or better still, a nice tack-on to his crouching combo chain.

Weapon XYZ - D,DB,B+PP:
-- Now he's the best there is at whatever Wolverine does! In an ode to
his Weapon X program brethren, Pool lets loose with his own stylish, slashy
autocombo fandango. The startup is short range and subtle, to either punish
with this from behind, or be creative with a juggle into it.

Yo-yo of Ultimate DOOM! - D,DB,B+KK:
-- Doom, doom, doomy doom doom doom, doomy doom doom doom, doomidy doom! 
Anyone else remember that show? Gir. Anyway, this is as it says - unleash
a ridiculous buzzsaw of pure win on the unsuspecting opponent. This is as
close as you get to your versus series projectile super, so savor the flavor!

Cable and Deadpool - D,DF,F+KK:
-- Nate? Nate? NAAAAAAAAAAAAAATE? Try not to create a time paradox when 
calling upon Nathan Dayspring Askani'son Summers Wesley Riblad the IVth in 
this one-of-a-kind team combo. Long windup on this one, but a great big payoff.
Try finishing off the opponent with it for a buddy comedy treat.

NINJAS! Ninjas? Oh, NINJAS!!! - D,DF,F+S:
-- Oh Bob, always getting into trouble... what a scamp. Anyway, this time the
trouble is with ninjas, and while Wade has a teleporty do-dad and Bob is a pretty fast
runner for a grunt, the opponent isn't so lucky. Despite the flood of ninjas involved
in this super, it's best use is as a defensive tactic to clip unsuspecting rushdowners.
