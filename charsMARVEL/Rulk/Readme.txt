Red Hulk or Rulk (Ross Thunderbolt) By Cravd, AJrenalin & Colosse for Mugen 1.0 & above
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The General Ross is the guy who wants to erradicate the Hulk, he got a transfusion from Hulk's blood & become himself an Hulk-type monster, but with red skin. He has some fire-related powers too.

Commands
--------

fire gun: ~D, DF, F, x or ~D,B,x

gamma charge (can be done in air too) : ~D, DF, F, y (vertical one is ~D, DB, B, y)

gamma quake: ~D, DF, F, z

super throw 1: ~D, B, a

super throw 2: ~D, B, b


Chemical barrel throw: ~D, DB, B, (x+y or x+z or y+z)

Machine gun: ~D, DF, F, (x+y or x+z or y+z)

hyper fury: ~D, DB, B, (a+b or b+c or a+c)

cosmic charge: ~D, DF, F, (a+b or b+c or a+c)

raging explosion: ~D, DF, F, (z+c or x+a or y+b)
