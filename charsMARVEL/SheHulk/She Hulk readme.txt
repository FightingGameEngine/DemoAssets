Specials
========
D, DF, F + P - Gamma Charge
D, DB, B + P - Gamma Bodyslam
D, DF, F + K - Flying Gamma Charge
D, DB, B + K - Gamma Kick (doable in air too)
B + K - Flip Kick

Hypers
======
D, DF, F + PP - Gamma Smackdown
D, DB, B + PP - Road Rage
D, DF, F + KK - Hyper Flip Kick
