JUBILEE BY GLADIACLOUD AND BEXIMUS

Move List:

Special moves

Firework: D,DF,F Punch (Air Ok)
Multiple Fireworks: B,DB,D,DF,F Punch
Popping Attack: D,D Punch
Jolt Flare: D,DB,B Punch
Rising Shine: F,D,DF Punch (press Punch repeatedly for more hits)
Freestyle Kick: F,D,DF,F Kick
Reverse Flash: D,DB,B Kick
Spark Trail: D,DF,F Kick (air only)

Super moves

Starmine: D,DF,F 2 Punches (Air Ok)
Big Bang Burst: D,DB,B 2 Punches
Go Time: D,DF,F 2 Kicks
